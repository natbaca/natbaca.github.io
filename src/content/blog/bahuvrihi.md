Related: Bahuvrihi - a type of compound word that denotes a referent by specifying a certain characteristic or quality the referent possesses…from Sanskrit: बहुव्रीहि, 'having much rice', originally referring to fertile land but later denoting the quality of being wealthy or rich. See English “lowlife”
