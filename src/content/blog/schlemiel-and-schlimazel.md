A schlemiel is somebody who often spills his soup, and a schlimazel is the person it lands on.

> **[Schlemiel - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schlemiel?wprov=sfti1#Meaning)**

“A schlemiel, a schlimazel and a schmendrik are irredeemably what they are. A schmuck can be enlightened. He can acquire, through a painful process of self-examination, the moral and social understanding that he has been missing. He can become, to revert to Wex’s dichotomy, a mentsh.”
