Can we bring back the diminutive “-ga” suffix?

-ga, Old English-\
Diminutive suffix, used especially in familiar names for pets.\
docga ― dog\
frocga ― frog\
\*picga ― pig\
\*snecga ― snail\
stacga ― stag\
wicga ― insect
