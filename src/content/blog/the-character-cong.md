TIL: The Chinese character 聰, meaning intelligent, is comprised of 耳 (ear) + 悤 (smarts).

悤 , in turn, is 囪 (window) and 心 (heart): “a mind as clear and transparent as a window”.

The original meaning was “ears that hear very clearly”.
