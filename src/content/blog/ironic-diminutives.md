TIL: ironic diminutives!

-ón, Spanish: usually embiggens, but in a few cases, indicates small size of or a lack of something (such as an ironic augmentative)\
‎pelo + ‎-ón → ‎pelón (“with little or no hair”)\
‎rabo + ‎-ón → ‎rabón (“with little or no tail”)\
‎rata (“rat”) + ‎-ón → ‎ratón (“mouse”)
