the coconut was named after a ghost demon!:\
"\[it\] wants to resemble a nose placed between two round eyes, from where it throws the sprout, when it wants to be born; by reason of such figure, it was called by our \[men\] coco, name imposed by the women on anything they want to put fear to the children"
